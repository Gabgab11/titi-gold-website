Meet Titi Gold

A woman of faith,
leadership and purpose.

Ogundele Mojisola Titilayo Wuraola Abosede, popularly known as Titi Gold, is an educator, project manager, author, humanitarian, and Christian leader passionate about transforming lives and advancing meaningful causes.

A graduate of Obafemi Awolowo University, she brings together a passion for learning, an appreciation for effective leadership, a heart for service, and an enduring commitment to her Christian faith.
